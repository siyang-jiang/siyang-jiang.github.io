CUHK-X

CUHK-X brings together multimodal sensing, datasets, and benchmarks to study how people act in everyday environments. The goal is to help models recognize actions, understand their context, and reason about what may happen next.

The dataset contains 64,267 samples of 40 actions from 30 participants across two indoor environments. It combines seven synchronized modalities: RGB, depth, infrared, thermal, IMU, mmWave radar, and skeleton. Its benchmarks cover human action recognition (HAR), understanding (HAU), and reasoning (HARn).
